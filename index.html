About Search Surveys

Has someone already asked this?

Many of us run surveys within our research projects, and most of them include far more questions than we ever have time to analyse. At the same time, many researchers, especially PhD students, have research questions but no funding to field their own survey.

Survey Finder brings the questionnaires of these project surveys together in one searchable place. Its aim is simple: to let you find out whether someone in our network has already collected the data you need, and to make it easy to get in touch to use it or to collaborate.

How it works

1

Search for the independent and dependent variables you need, by category, by subcategory or with your own words in English or Spanish.

2

See which surveys measure both, with the exact question wording and response scales.

3

Contact the owner to ask for permission to use the data, or to propose a collaboration.

Only questionnaires, never data

The site lists questionnaires and codebooks only. The data always stays with the researcher or project that collected it. Each owner decides who can use it, how it should be cited and whether they want to collaborate. Every survey shows one of three access statuses:

OpenThe data can be downloaded from the link provided. Cite it as the owner indicates.
On requestWrite to the contact person to ask for permission to use the data.
EmbargoedThe data is not available yet, but you can get in touch about collaborating.

Owners can also mark their survey as open to collaboration, whatever its access status.

How to cite

Each survey shows the reference its owners want cited: a data paper, the first publication using the data, or a DOI. Owners who don't have one yet can get a free DOI by depositing their questionnaire on Zenodo.

Add your survey

Fill in the contribution form and attach your questionnaire or codebook (PDF, Word, Excel, or SPSS/Stata files with labels). We take care of adding it to the site. You decide the access status, how your data should be cited, and whether the full questionnaire can be downloaded.
