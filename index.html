Survey Finder

Questionnaires and codebooks from our research projects, in one searchable place. Enter the independent and dependent variables you need and see which surveys measure both, then contact the owners to use the data or to collaborate. Only questionnaires are listed here; the data always stays with its owners.

Access statuses

  • Open — the data can be downloaded from the link on the card. Cite it as the owner indicates.
  • On request — write to the contact person to ask for permission to use the data.
  • Embargoed — the data is not available yet, but you can get in touch about collaborating.

"Open to collaboration" is shown separately: owners can welcome co-authors whatever the access status.

Add your survey

Fill in the contribution form and attach the questionnaire or codebook (PDF, Word, Excel, or SPSS/Stata files with labels). Only the questionnaire is published, never the data.

You decide the access status, how your data should be cited, and whether the full questionnaire can be downloaded from this site.

No citation yet?

If you don't have a data paper or a first publication to cite, you can get a free DOI by depositing your questionnaire on Zenodo. It takes about ten minutes and the DOI belongs to you.

Survey Finder is maintained by volunteers from the Young Researchers in Gender and Social Sciences network. Questionnaire content belongs to each project.
